# BARKVERSE Project State

## Locked product thesis

**What if dogs had their own internet?**

The product is funny, entertaining, emotional, child-friendly, dog-lover friendly, and technically credible.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Product quality before prize-category checkboxes.
2. Every prize technology must have a genuine product role.
3. Core experience remains usable without paid infrastructure.
4. No speculative token economy.
5. Private memories/photos stay off-chain.
6. OpenRouter is fallback infrastructure, not a replacement for the Google AI prize integration.
7. Vercel deployments are batched to conserve Hobby deployment quota.
8. No feature is allowed to threaten the submission deadline.
