# Judging Alignment

## Relevance to Theme

Dogs are the entire product world: identity, news, missions, investigations, games, voices and memories.

## Creativity

The premise is an alternate internet/civilization run by dogs rather than a conventional pet tracker, breed detector or chatbot.

## Technical Execution

The MVP is a real interactive web application with a short, reliable user journey.

## Writing Quality

The submission story is built around one memorable question:

> What if dogs had their own internet?

## Prize Technology

### Google AI
Core multimodal intelligence and personalization.

### ElevenLabs
Dog characters speak generated responses.

### Solana
Pawprint creates a verifiable proof for a meaningful memory.

### Snowflake
Anonymized aggregate events power Barkverse Observatory.

## Integrity rule

Technology must solve a real product problem. No superficial integrations.
